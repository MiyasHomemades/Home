Why Kavuni

An heirloom rice, once reserved for royalty.

Kavuni arisi is a rare purple-black rice from Tamil Nadu, prized for centuries and nearly forgotten. We source it directly from small farms and stone-grind it in-house — nothing added, nothing stripped away.
